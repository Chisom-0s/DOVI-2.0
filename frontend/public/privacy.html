Data Protection

Privacy Policy

How Dovi protects and respects your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, purchase products, or interact with Dovi, we collect:

  • Account details: Name, email address, phone number, and campus affiliation.
  • Delivery information: Delivery address, hostel name, campus, state, and recipient contact.
  • Transactional data: Order history, escrow transaction records, and communication logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process orders, verify payments, and route deliveries through our logistics network.
  • To provide real-time transactional email notifications via Resend and live chat support via Smartsupp.
  • To prevent fraudulent transactions and safeguard the campus marketplace community.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Delivery information is shared only with the assigned logistics carriers solely for parcel dispatch. All payment details are processed under PCI-DSS compliant encryption.

4. Your Data Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your account and personal information at any time by contacting privacy@dovishop.com or support@dovishop.com.
