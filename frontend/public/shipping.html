Shipping & Campus Delivery Policy

Fast, reliable, and consolidated fulfillment for tertiary institutions.

1. Order Fulfillment & Consolidation

Dovi operates a centralized logistics fulfillment model. Orders placed from one or multiple vendors are routed to our Onitsha Fulfillment Hub where items are consolidated into single parcels. This eliminates duplicate delivery charges and ensures all items arrive safely.

2. Delivery Classifications

To keep shipping rates fair for students, items on Dovi are categorized into 4 delivery classes:

  • Small Parcel (under 2 kg): Gadgets, accessories, cosmetics, clothing, clippers, books. Fast express campus dispatch.
  • Medium Parcel (2 kg to 10 kg): Home appliances, electronics, audio gear, kitchen equipment.
  • Bulky Parcel (10 kg to 30 kg): Study tables, washing machines, compact refrigerators, TVs.
  • Heavy Freight: Furniture suites and large commercial appliances requiring specialized van transport.

3. Delivery Timelines

  • Intra-City & Onitsha Zone: 24 to 48 hours.
  • Regional Campus Delivery (South-East & South-South): 24 to 48 hours.
  • Nationwide Campus Terminals (Lagos, Abuja, West & North): 48 to 72 hours.

4. Campus Pickup Stations

In addition to doorstep delivery, Dovi coordinates with verified student campus ambassadors and central campus pickup points (e.g. Student Union buildings, commercial parks) where students can safely collect parcels between lectures.

5. Package Inspection Protocol

Upon receiving your package, you are entitled to inspect the contents. Once delivered, your 48-hour Dovi Inspection Window commences, during which your payment remains safely protected in escrow.
