Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about shopping, payments, and delivery on Dovi.

1. What is Dovi and how does it work?
Dovi is a centralized student-focused marketplace in Nigeria. You can browse verified electronics, fashion, home essentials, and auto supplies. When you place an order, your payment is held securely in escrow until you receive and confirm your purchase.
2. Is my money safe when paying on Dovi?
Yes, 100%. Dovi uses an escrow payment system. The vendor does not receive your payment when you checkout. Funds are only released to the vendor after your parcel is delivered and you have completed your 48-hour inspection period without dispute.
3. What payment methods are accepted?
Dovi accepts Nigerian debit cards (Mastercard, Visa, Verve), direct bank transfers, mobile bank USSD, and Dovi wallet balances. All payment processing is encrypted with bank-grade 256-bit SSL.
4. How long does campus delivery take?
Standard campus delivery typically takes 24 to 72 hours depending on your campus location. Deliveries routed through our Onitsha Fulfillment Hub are consolidated to reduce delivery costs and speed up transit.
5. What is the Save2Own program?
Save2Own allows students to save toward high-value items (such as laptops, smartphones, and home appliances) through interest-free micro-payments. Once your goal target is reached, the product is fulfilled and delivered to you immediately with 0% interest and zero hidden charges.
6. Can I return a product if it's damaged or not as described?
Yes. Dovi offers a 48-hour inspection window from the moment of delivery. If the item is defective, damaged, or does not match the vendor's listing, you can open a dispute or refund request directly from your dashboard for an immediate replacement or full escrow refund.
7. How do I track my order?
You will receive an automated email confirmation with your unique order tracking reference (e.g. DOVI-ORD-XXXX). You can view live dispatch updates from your Order History page or contact our support team.
8. How can I sell on Dovi as a vendor?
Verified vendors can apply to sell on Dovi by registering a vendor account and completing business and identity verification. Once approved, vendors can list catalog products and manage fulfillment through our centralized dispatch system.
9. How do I contact customer support?
You can reach Dovi customer support via live chat (Smartsupp widget on the bottom right of any page), by emailing support@dovishop.com or hello@dovishop.com, or during business hours (8:00 AM – 8:00 PM WAT).
