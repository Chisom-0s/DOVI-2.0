Buyer Protection

Returns, Refunds & Dispute Policy

100% Escrow-backed buyer protection on every order.

1. The 48-Hour Inspection Guarantee

Every buyer on Dovi is protected by our centralized escrow system. From the exact timestamp a parcel is marked as delivered, buyers have 48 hours to inspect their purchase and verify that it matches the description, functions properly, and arrived undamaged.

2. Eligible Reasons for Return & Full Refund

  • The item is defective, faulty, or does not power on.
  • The item differs substantially from the specifications or photos displayed on Dovi.
  • The wrong size, color, or model was dispatched.
  • The parcel arrived physically broken or damaged during transit.
  • Counterfeit or unverified goods reported within the inspection period.

3. How to Initiate a Return

  1. Navigate to your Order History in your Dovi account.
  2. Select the specific order and click "Request Refund / Open Dispute".
  3. Upload clear photos or a short video demonstrating the issue along with a brief description.
  4. Dovi's mediation team will review the claim within 12 hours.

4. Escrow Refund Disbursement

Because the vendor does not hold your funds during the inspection window, approved refunds are processed directly by Dovi. Funds are returned immediately to your Dovi wallet or original bank account within 24 to 48 hours of item return confirmation.
