Company Overview

About DoviShop

Dovi — Shop Smart. Feel Good.

Dovi is Nigeria's premier student-focused centralized marketplace designed to make campus shopping secure, affordable, and seamless. We empower tertiary students, young professionals, and campus communities to access high-quality goods from verified vendors without fear of fraud or disappointment.

A Centralized Marketplace Model

Unlike unmoderated classifieds or peer-to-peer bulletin boards where buyers and sellers transact at their own risk, Dovi centrally manages and guarantees every single transaction:

  • 100% Escrow Protection: Every payment made on Dovi is held in secure escrow. Funds are never disbursed to the vendor until the student receives, inspects, and approves their item.
  • Vendor Verification: Every vendor on Dovi undergoes strict identity and quality verification before listing products.
  • Centralized Customer Operations: If an issue arises with an order, Dovi's dedicated resolution team intervenes directly to provide refunds, replacements, or support.
  • Consolidated Logistics: Through our flagship Onitsha Fulfillment Hub and campus dispatch stations, orders from multiple vendors are consolidated into single parcels, saving students substantial delivery fees.

Our Core Values

  • Student-First Pricing: Fair, transparent market pricing negotiated directly with manufacturers and verified wholesalers.
  • Save2Own Financial Inclusion: Innovative zero-interest micro-installment savings program that allows students to acquire essential laptops, electronics, and study tools without predatory loans.
  • Speed & Reliability: Direct campus deliveries and designated campus pick-up points across Nigerian universities and polytechnics within 24 to 72 hours.
