Frequently Asked Questions

Instant answers to everything you need to know about shopping, delivery, and payments on Dovi.

What is Dovi?

Dovi (https://dovishop.com) is Nigeria's premier centralized student and campus marketplace, operating under the brand tagline 'Dovi — Shop Smart. Feel Good.' We connect verified vendors and student buyers with 100% escrow buyer protection, zero-interest Save2Own micro-installments, and fast campus fulfillment.

How does Dovi Escrow Buyer Protection work?

When you pay for an order on Dovi, your payment is held securely in our centralized escrow account. The vendor only receives payment after the package arrives, is inspected at our hub or delivery point, and you have confirmed satisfaction within the 48-hour inspection period. If the item is defective or not as described, your payment is promptly refunded.

What is Save2Own on Dovi?

Save2Own is Dovi's zero-interest micro-installment savings program designed for students. It allows you to lock in the price of essential items (such as laptops, smartphones, home electronics, and course equipment) and pay in flexible daily, weekly, or monthly installments. Once fully funded, the product is dispatched directly to your campus.

How does shipping and campus delivery work?

Dovi operates a centralized fulfillment model with its primary consolidation hub in Onitsha, Anambra State. Small parcels and electronics arrive at campus delivery hubs within 24 to 72 hours, while standard regional parcels typically take 2 to 5 business days. Real-time tracking is provided for every order.

What is the return and refund policy?

Buyers enjoy a 48-hour inspection window upon package receipt. If an item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, you can initiate a return through your Dovi dashboard or by emailing support@dovishop.com. Returned items are inspected at our hub, and approved refunds are credited back via your original payment method or Dovi wallet.

How do I contact Dovi customer support?

You can reach Dovi Customer Care by email at hello@dovishop.com or support@dovishop.com, through our website live chat powered by Smartsupp at https://dovishop.com, or via our customer support hotline during business hours (Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM WAT).

Can students and vendors sell their products on Dovi?

Yes! Dovi welcomes verified independent merchants, brand distributors, and student entrepreneurs. Vendors undergo identity and store verification, list products in accordance with our quality guidelines, and receive automated weekly payouts once deliveries are confirmed.
